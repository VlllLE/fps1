## Ghillie suit

No free-licensed ghillie-suit character exists on the sources searched (see below). The `Ghillie` look is therefore the
"Solider" model (boonie hat, camouflage uniform) with a ghillie suit generated by `Tools/Blender/real_characters/ghillie.py`:
1500 tapered strips in four olive, moss, brown and tan materials, scattered over the shoulders, back, arms and legs
and skinned to the same bones as the body underneath. The strips are original work for this project (no third-party
asset) and fall under the repository's own license.

## What was changed from the downloads

The downloaded models are not used as shipped. `Tools/Blender/real_characters/retarget.py` (run in Blender 4.5):

- maps each model's own skeleton onto the game's 19 humanoid bones (Hips, Spine, Chest, Neck, Head, both shoulders, arms,
  forearms, hands, thighs, shins and feet) and re-skins the meshes onto them in the game's rest pose;
- scales the model to the game's character proportions (arm span, leg length) and stands it on the origin;
- removes loose props that are not part of the body (stray spheres, weapons and so on);
- downsizes the colour textures to 1024 px (`Soldier_albedo.png`, `Ghillie_albedo.png`); the Operator keeps its per-material colours and has no texture;
- exports the result as `Operator.fbx`, `Soldier.fbx` and `Ghillie.fbx`.

The original GLB downloads are kept for reproducibility in `Source~/` (a folder Unity ignores), next to the measured
model heights (`*.height.txt`). `Assets/Editor/CharacterLookSetup.cs` (menu Tools > Characters > Setup Real Soldier Looks)
configures the FBX importers and lists the looks in `Assets/Resources/Characters/SoldierModelSet.asset`.

## Where the models were looked for

- Poly Pizza (https://poly.pizza): all three models above. Its listing shows the license of every model; only CC0 and CC-BY were considered.
- Quaternius (https://quaternius.com): Poly Pizza hosts the Quaternius characters used here; no ghillie suit.
- Kenney (https://kenney.nl): nothing taken; the Poly Pizza models fitted the brief and Kenney's free character packs are stylised figures rather than tactical soldiers.
- OpenGameArt (https://opengameart.org): search results were low quality or non-humanoid.
- itch.io: answers 403 to non-browser clients, so it could not be searched without a login.
- Sketchfab: ghillie models there need a login to download, so none were used.

The first-person arms are still the original Blender `SoldierArmsFP` model; only their sleeve and glove colours follow the look.
